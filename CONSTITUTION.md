# ThreadLab Constitution

---

## Article I

ThreadLab exists to reduce the distance between an idea and perfect embroidery.

Every decision we make must move us closer to that mission.

If a feature makes the software more complex without making embroidery easier, it does not belong in ThreadLab.

---

### Our Principles

1. Simplicity over features.

2. Craftsmanship over speed.

3. Confidence over automation.
